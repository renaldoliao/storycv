FAQ

Questions? We got you.

What does “ATS-friendly” mean?

Many companies use hiring software (called an ATS) to scan CVs before a person reads them. Fancy layouts can confuse it. StoryCV uses a clean, one-column layout with real text, so the software can read every line.

Can I chat in Bahasa Indonesia?

Yes! Pick English or Bahasa Indonesia when you set up, and your buddy will chat and write your CV in that language.

Will it make things up about me?

No. Your buddy only uses what you tell it. If something is unclear, it asks a follow-up question instead of guessing.

Which buddy should I pick?

Whoever feels right! Kira is great if you want a confidence boost, Kopi if you like taking your time, and Tobi if you want it quick and fun. They all build the same quality CV.

How long does it take?

Most people finish the chat in about 10 minutes. You can stop anytime and come back later.

Why do I need an account?

Your account keeps your chat and CV with you, lets you come back to finish later, and helps us keep the service fair for everyone.
