FAQ

Questions? We got you.

Ask away! No question is too small.
What does “ATS-friendly” mean?

Most companies use software (an ATS) to store and search CVs. An ATS-friendly CV uses a clean layout and real text, so the software can read every line and recruiters can find you. Learn more about ATS.

Is chatting really free?

Yes. You can chat, see your CV lines appear, and preview the top of your CV for free. You only pay when you want the full CV and the PDF.

How do I pay?

With QRIS or an e-wallet. Your CV unlocks right away, with no waiting for an admin to check your payment.

Can I use Bahasa Indonesia?

Yes. Choose English or Bahasa Indonesia, and your buddy chats and writes your CV in that language.

Will it make things up about me?

No. Your buddy only uses what you tell it. If something is unclear, it asks a follow-up question instead of guessing.

Which buddy should I pick?

Whoever feels right! Kira for a confidence boost, Kopi if you like taking your time, Tobi if you want it quick and fun. They all build the same quality CV. Meet the buddies.

How long does it take?

Most people finish the chat in about 10 minutes. You can stop anytime and come back later.

Why do I need an account?

So your chat and CV are saved, you can come back later, and the service stays fair for everyone.
